The Problem

Slow ordering

The Solution

A tireless AI waiter at every table

Guests scan a QR code and instantly chat — by text or voice — with an AI that knows your full menu, suggests the perfect dishes, handles allergies, and routes requests to your staff.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to buy any hardware? +

No. TalkTable runs entirely on your guests' own smartphones via a QR code. There's nothing to install on-site.

How long does setup take? +

Most restaurants go live in under 24 hours. We import your menu, generate branded QR codes, and onboard your staff.

Which languages are supported? +

TalkTable natively supports Arabic and English, with more languages on the roadmap.

How does pricing work? +

Start with a 14-day free trial. The Starter plan is just JOD 0.10 per order. Enterprise pricing is custom for chains.

Can it handle allergies and dietary needs? +

Yes. The AI flags allergens and recommends dishes that match each guest's dietary requirements.

Will it replace my waiters? +

No — it empowers them. TalkTable handles routine ordering and upselling so your staff can focus on hospitality.

Can guests still call a human waiter? +

Absolutely. The built-in waiter request system lets guests reach your staff instantly with one tap.
